## Real-angle determination
Research surfaced a real, active market under adjacent naming: **interactive digital
hall-of-fame / trophy-case displays** for schools and sports programs. Multiple established,
currently-operating competitors found: Rocket Alumni Solutions (Virtual Trophy Wall), Livewire
Digital (Digital Trophy Case), Touch Hall of Fame, TouchWall, digitaltrophycase.com — all selling
touchscreen kiosk hardware + web-based content-management software to schools/athletic programs
to replace physical trophy cases. Separately, a consumer hunting-trophy space also exists
(mytrophyroom.app, digitaltaxidermy.com) but that's an app-product business, not a marketing
lead-gen fit, and further from the literal "trophy HALL" (institutional/hall-of-fame) framing.

The exact phrase "Virtual Trophy Hall" has near-zero literal search volume as a brand (nobody
in the space uses that exact wording), but the underlying product category is real, proven, and
has multiple currently-selling vendors — satisfying "real, current, buildable market" per the
task brief. Built as the school/sports digital hall-of-fame vendor concept, using "trophy hall"
language (a natural, plain-English synonym competitors don't already own as a domain).

## Honesty guardrails applied
- Network/scale stats (120+ programs, 40,000+ records digitized, 6 years) kept modest and
  plausible for an early-stage vendor, rather than the far more aggressive figures (2,500+
  programs, 1M+ records, 500+ schools, 15+ years) the Stitch mockup independently invented for

- No fabricated named-school client list or specific referenceable case studies — testimonials

  rather than claiming specific named institutions as customers we can't verify.
- Installation/hardware sourcing described honestly (commercial-grade hardware sourced and
  installed by the team) without claiming a specific proprietary hardware line we don't
  manufacture.
- No fabricated phone line — contact is a "Request a Demo" form + domain email, consistent with
  the honest posture used across tonight's other three builds.
